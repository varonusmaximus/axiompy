# Acme Shoe Care Guide

## Cleaning Your Shoes

### General Cleaning Steps
1. Remove laces and insoles
2. Use a soft brush to remove loose dirt
3. Mix mild soap with warm water
4. Gently scrub the upper with a soft cloth
5. Wipe clean with a damp cloth
6. Air dry away from direct heat

### Material-Specific Care

**Leather (Air Force 1, Dunk)**
- Use leather cleaner and conditioner
- Avoid soaking the leather
- Apply leather protector spray after cleaning

**Mesh/Flyknit**
- Can be cleaned with soap and water
- For tough stains, use a soft brush
- Machine wash on delicate is possible but not recommended

**Suede**
- Use a suede brush only
- Never use water on suede
- Apply suede protector spray

## Storage Tips

- Store in a cool, dry place
- Use shoe trees to maintain shape
- Keep in original box or dust bags
- Avoid stacking heavy items on top
- Rotate between multiple pairs to extend lifespan

## When to Replace Your Shoes

### Running Shoes
Replace every 300-500 miles. Signs it's time:
- Midsole feels compressed or flat
- Outsole tread is worn smooth
- Upper has holes or tears
- You notice new aches or pains while running

### Basketball Shoes
Replace when traction pattern is worn down or ankle support feels loose.

### Lifestyle Shoes
Replace when they look worn out or feel uncomfortable. With proper care, quality leather shoes can last years.
