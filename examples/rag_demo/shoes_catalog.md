# Acme Shoe Catalog

## Running Shoes

### Acme Air Zoom Pegasus 40
The Pegasus 40 is Acme's most popular running shoe. It features responsive Zoom Air cushioning in the forefoot and React foam in the midsole for a smooth, bouncy ride. The engineered mesh upper provides breathability and support. Price: $130. Available in men's and women's sizes.

### Acme Vaporfly 3
The Vaporfly 3 is Acme's elite marathon racing shoe. It features a full-length carbon fiber plate embedded in ZoomX foam for maximum energy return. The shoe has helped athletes break world records. Price: $260. Recommended for race day only.

### Acme Invincible 3
The Invincible 3 is designed for maximum cushioning on daily training runs. It uses ZoomX foam throughout the entire midsole, providing the softest ride in Acme's lineup. Great for recovery runs. Price: $180.

## Basketball Shoes

### Acme LeBron 21
The LeBron 21 features Zoom Air cushioning and a reinforced heel for stability during quick cuts. The shoe is designed for powerful players who need support and responsiveness. Price: $200.

### Acme KD 16
Kevin Durant's signature shoe focuses on court feel and responsiveness. It has a lower profile than the LeBron and uses a full-length Zoom Strobel for energy return. Price: $160.

## Lifestyle Shoes

### Acme Air Force 1
The classic Air Force 1 is Acme's best-selling lifestyle shoe. Originally designed for basketball in 1982, it features Air cushioning and a durable leather upper. Available in low, mid, and high-top versions. Price: $110.

### Acme Dunk Low
The Dunk has become a fashion icon. Originally a basketball shoe from 1985, it's now one of the most sought-after sneakers for everyday wear. Features padded collar and classic Acme styling. Price: $110.
