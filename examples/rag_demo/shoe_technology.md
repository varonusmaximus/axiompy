# Acme Shoe Technology Guide

## Cushioning Technologies

### ZoomX Foam
ZoomX is Acme's lightest and most responsive foam. It's made from Pebax, a material that provides exceptional energy return (up to 85%). Used in elite racing shoes like the Vaporfly and Alphafly. The foam is super soft but bouncy, perfect for fast-paced running.

### React Foam
React foam offers a balance of softness, responsiveness, and durability. It's more durable than ZoomX but slightly heavier. Found in the Pegasus and many training shoes. Great for everyday runners who need long-lasting cushioning.

### Air Technology
Acme Air has been around since 1979. Pressurized air units in the sole provide lightweight cushioning that doesn't compress over time. Types include:
- Air Max: Visible air units for maximum cushioning
- Zoom Air: Tensioned fibers for responsive, bouncy feel
- Air Sole: Classic cushioning found in the Air Force 1

## Upper Technologies

### Flyknit
Flyknit is Acme's precision-engineered knit upper technology. It provides a sock-like fit that's lightweight and breathable. Each zone can be engineered for specific properties - stretchy where you need flexibility, supportive where you need stability.

### Engineered Mesh
Traditional mesh that's designed with different densities in different areas. More affordable than Flyknit but still provides good breathability and support. Found in most running shoes.

## Plate Technologies

### Carbon Fiber Plate
Full-length carbon fiber plates are used in racing shoes to improve running economy. The plate acts as a lever that helps propel the runner forward. Found in Vaporfly, Alphafly, and some competitor shoes.

### Midfoot Shank
A semi-rigid plate in the midfoot area that provides stability without the aggressive propulsion of a full carbon plate. Good for daily training shoes.

## Sizing Guide

Acme shoes generally run true to size. However:
- Running shoes: Consider going half size up for toe room
- Basketball shoes: Go true to size for a locked-in feel
- Lifestyle shoes: True to size works for most people

For wide feet, look for shoes marked "Wide" or with a 2E/4E width designation.
